# Signal Ledger V3 — Screenshot Critic Round 3

> Review mode: screenshot-only manual critic. The current harness has no independent subagent context, so this is not represented as an independent-model review. The stability decision below is based on the fresh Round-3 browser screenshots only.

## Visual-language diagnosis

The design has crossed from “directionally interesting” into a stable editorial system. Home, Article, Archive, Search, Dark, and Mobile now share one visual grammar: warm paper/ink, mono registration metadata, restrained vermilion, and the Signal Rail as the sole signature device. The remaining work is reduction and code cleanup rather than another visual concept pass.

## 3 serious problems checked

1. **No top navigation reappeared.** The first screen enters the content system directly; utilities remain embedded in the document rather than forming a page header.
2. **Desktop article H1 no longer overwhelms the reading frame.** The title now holds a compact multi-line block and leaves visible space for article metadata, TOC, and body entry.
3. **Mobile article geometry remains intact.** The H1 uses the available content width instead of collapsing into a narrow column, and the mobile TOC/meta/body sequence remains readable.

No material new visual defect was found in the representative Round-3 screenshots.

## 3 strengths

1. **Signal Rail is now sufficient as the identity.** Nothing else competes with it as a signature motif.
2. **Chinese long-form reading is credible.** The article body has a clear 700px-class desktop measure, strong mobile line length, and a visible-but-secondary context rail.
3. **The site is quiet without becoming generic.** Archive chronology, search results, and writing ledger all feel related without relying on cards, gradients, rounded containers, or dashboard chrome.

## 5 final corrections represented in this round

1. Simplify the homepage utility row from slash-heavy navigation-like labels to a compact tools record.
2. Reduce desktop article H1 maximum size.
3. Reduce and balance desktop article H1 measure so Chinese line breaks stay editorial rather than poster-like.
4. Increase context TOC link size to a genuinely scannable level.
5. Tighten TOC line-height while preserving its subordinate role.

## Stability decision

**Proceed to Deliver reduction.** A Round 4 is not justified: the current screenshots do not show strong template/AI/SaaS influence, poor Chinese reading, or a material mobile defect. Additional visual changes would now be more likely to add noise than fix a real problem.

## Score

**8.0 / 10** — stable enough for production reduction and final verification; remaining work is cleanup, accessibility/performance proof, and merge-readiness review rather than visual reinvention.
